# GoreeCloud Calendar — Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Calendar aims to unify the independent strengths of general-purpose personal scheduling, delegated work coordination, family collaboration and private self-hosted calendars.

- Match the common calendar/event/task capabilities of established scheduling products.
- Add advanced availability, meeting scheduling and appointment bookings without mandatory external platforms.
- Deliver event discussions and group coordination without adding photo albums.
- Prioritize explicit private sharing, retention, data ownership and secure client-server operations.
- Build open-standard ICS/CalDAV portability and provider adapters only where legitimately supported.
- Reach functional parity across the web, Linux and Android clients through verified native packaging.
- Support self-hosting with low maintenance overhead, testable recovery and no mandatory recurring fees.
- Integrate with GC UI, GoreeCloud Mesh, GoreeCloud Identity, GoreeShield and Wardveil without claiming nonexistent integrations.

These are future competitive goals, not claims about features already deployed.
